## Approved Learn More copy

Use the following popup titles and paragraphs verbatim, except for the specifically approved `initial programs` correction in item 8. Preserve paragraph breaks and punctuation.

### 1. SoCal Gas

A brand-new opportunity in the Southern California natural gas market. Customers can choose an alternative natural gas supplier while continuing to receive their gas service through their existing utility.

This is an easy program to introduce because virtually every eligible gas customer is a potential prospect — homeowners and renters alike. There is no equipment to install and no complicated change to the customer’s home.

For agents, the earning potential can be substantial, with an average agent having the opportunity to earn up to $1,000 per day.

### 2. Community Solar

Help Southern California businesses lower their electricity costs while supporting clean, renewable energy.

Through our Community Solar program, qualifying businesses can receive a guaranteed minimum 10% savings compared with their current Southern California Edison costs — without purchasing or installing solar panels.

Businesses simply subscribe to energy generated by a community solar farm and receive the savings. Because each solar farm has limited capacity, there is a strong reason for businesses to enroll while space is still available.

For agents, this is a high-value opportunity, with average commissions of approximately $450 per completed sale.

### 3. Community Health Worker Program

Turn helping people in your community into an additional source of income.

Agents can become certified as Community Health Workers and help Medi-Cal recipients learn about additional benefits, services and community resources that may be available to them.

The program is especially attractive for agents already working in communities with large Medi-Cal populations because it can be offered alongside other programs they may already be presenting.

It’s an opportunity to help people access valuable resources while getting paid for the work you perform.

### 4. BridgeLink360

Turn the people you already know into another source of income.

BridgeLink360 gives agents the opportunity to earn money by referring friends, family members and customers who are looking to purchase electronics and wireless products.

There is no cost to join, making it an easy supplemental opportunity for agents who want to add another income stream without taking away from the programs they are already selling.

### 5. Lifeline

Help eligible consumers stay connected while earning income through one of the country’s most established telecommunications assistance programs.

Lifeline provides qualifying low-income consumers with discounted phone service. Agents help eligible customers understand the program, determine whether they qualify and complete the enrollment process.

For agents who enjoy working directly in their communities, Lifeline provides another opportunity to help customers receive an important everyday service while creating income at the same time.

### 6. Mobile Health Testing

Bring important health testing directly into the communities you already serve — and get paid for helping make it available.

Our Mobile Health Testing programs can include testing for UTIs, STIs, COVID, RSV, flu and comprehensive Respiratory Pathogen Panels (RPP), with additional testing programs added as they become available.

Rather than selling a traditional product, agents help connect eligible individuals with convenient diagnostic testing that can identify common infections and respiratory illnesses.

For agents already working face-to-face with large numbers of people, Mobile Health Testing can create an entirely new income opportunity while providing a valuable healthcare service to the community.

### 7. Customer Rewards

Turn an ordinary offer into something customers get excited about.

Our Customer Rewards program allows you to incentivize customers to take an action by giving them valuable travel experiences for free. Depending on the campaign, you can offer customers free airfare, free cruises, free hotel stays or free vacation packages simply for completing the desired action.

Use these rewards to encourage customers to make a purchase, sign up for a service, attend an appointment, request a demonstration, refer a friend or take virtually any other action you want to promote.

Instead of simply asking a customer to say yes, you’re giving them an exciting reason to do it — at a cost to you that is only a fraction of the perceived value of the reward.

### 8. New Opportunities

R8 is built around opportunity — and our initial programs are only the beginning.

We continually search for new products, services and programs that can give our agents additional ways to earn income. As new opportunities become available, R8 agents can be among the first to learn about them and decide which programs are right for their business.

One relationship. Multiple opportunities. More ways to earn.
